Welcome to DSM

Connected systems.
Real-world impact.

We are the Distributed Systems Middleware Group at UC Irvine, led by Professor Nalini Venkatasubramanian.

Our research spans adaptive middleware, mobile and pervasive computing, quality-aware data management, and distributed multimedia. We explore how these systems can support smart, resilient communities.
